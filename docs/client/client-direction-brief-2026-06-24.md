## Overall direction

The goal is **not** a traditional gym website.

The goal is to create a **luxury hospitality, private club, and editorial brand experience** that happens to be centered around strength, health, and community.

The site should feel inspired by:

- Aimé Leon Dore's culture
- Soho House's membership model
- Aman's hospitality
- Switchyards' simplicity
- Buck Mason's masculinity
- NeueHouse's creative community

### The website should communicate

1. Who we are.
2. What we stand for.
3. Why we're different.
4. What the United ecosystem looks like.
5. How to apply for membership.

### The site should feel

Sophisticated · Minimal · Intentional · Editorial · Cinematic · Premium · Timeless · Warm, not corporate · Exclusive, but approachable

### The website's primary purpose is to

1. Showcase culture and experience.
2. Establish standards and positioning.
3. Create desire and curiosity.
4. Educate visitors on the ecosystem.
5. Drive applications.
