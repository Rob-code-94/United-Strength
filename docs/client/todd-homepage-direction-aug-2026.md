## North star

After looking through [Odd Ritual Golf](https://oddritualgolf.com), Todd wants the homepage to capture the **same feeling** — not a copy, but an editorial introduction to the brand. Visitors should slowly discover who United is rather than seeing everything immediately. The experience should feel like moving through an **editorial publication**, not scrolling a traditional gym website.

**Vintage / heritage editorial feel** is part of the mood reference alongside Odd Ritual's pacing and interaction.

---

## Homepage structure — two acts

The homepage has **two acts**:

1. **Act 1 — Editorial intro** — Four full-screen chapters (teasers only; not full content)
2. **Act 2 — Content sections** — Six sections with full layouts (Believe → Start Here)

Navigation, locked IA, and Culture Club tokens are unchanged. See [ai-studio-brief.md](ai-studio-brief.md) for locked nav.

---

## Act 1 — Editorial intro chapters

Instead of a single static hero, the homepage opens with a series of **full-screen editorial chapters** the visitor scrolls through. Each chapter reveals one idea at a time.

### Shared chapter anatomy

Each intro chapter includes:

- **Full-screen photograph** or **subtle looping video** (very slow, cinematic movement — not flashy)
- **Minimal typography**
- **Large chapter number** (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`)
- **Small preview of what's coming next** (Odd Ritual pattern)

### Progression

```
01 // United Strength Club
        ↓
02 // What We Believe

02 // What We Believe
        ↓
03 // The Four Pillars

03 // The Four Pillars
        ↓
04 // Experience United
```

These are **not** full content sections. They introduce each idea and encourage the visitor to continue scrolling.

Once the visitor finishes this opening sequence, the homepage transitions into the actual content (Act 2).

## Act 2 — Content sections

### Section 1 // What We Believe

This is where the story begins.

**Layout:**

- Large editorial headline
- Short supporting paragraph
- One strong supporting image
- Plenty of negative space
- Minimal call-to-action

**Focus:** Communicate why United exists — not explain everything we offer.

---

### Section 2 // The Four Pillars

Four **editorial feature panels** — not traditional cards.

**Pillars (order locked):**

1. **Foundation**
2. **Reflection**
3. **Longevity**
4. **Move the City**

Each panel includes:

- Icon
- Title
- One short supporting sentence
- Subtle interaction that encourages visitors to explore that section further

**Note:** Pillars represent foundational brand ideas. They do **not** mirror the navigation structure.

---

### Section 3 // Experience United

First **functional** section of the homepage. Communicate essentials in a clean, premium way — not paragraphs.

**Includes:**

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Classes | 5 |
| Duration | 14 Days |
| Price | $75 |
| Copy | Short explanation |
| CTA | Primary **Start Here** button |

Visual treatment should feel **elevated and intentional** — not promotional.

---

### Section 4 // The Space

Photography carries most of the emotion. Minimal copy.

**Photography focus:**

- Architecture
- Natural light
- Equipment
- Details
- Materials
- Members
- Small moments throughout the space

**Goal:** Communicate the feeling of being inside United — not simply show the gym.

---

### Section 5 // Membership

Homepage does **not** explain every membership option.

**Layout:**

- Large statement
- Short supporting paragraph
- Button leading to the Membership page

**Emphasis:** Belonging and community — **not pricing**. No public monthly prices on this layer.

---

### Section 6 // Start Here

Homepage concludes with two very clear paths:

1. **Experience United**
2. **Apply for Membership**

Make the next step obvious without overwhelming visitors with too many options.

## Overall design direction

The homepage should feel like moving through an editorial publication rather than a traditional gym website.

### Design principles

- Large immersive photography
- Subtle looping video where appropriate
- Minimal typography
- Strong use of white space
- Editorial layouts
- Slow, intentional pacing
- Clean transitions between sections
- Subtle animations and interactions
- Premium, timeless aesthetic

### Experience goal

Gradually reveal United as visitors scroll — creating curiosity and allowing them to understand who we are, what we believe, and what we offer **one idea at a time**.

People should feel like they're **exploring a thoughtfully designed brand experience** rather than simply browsing a fitness website.

## Reference — Odd Ritual Golf

**URL:** https://oddritualgolf.com

### Borrow

- Culture-first brand feel
- Numbered scroll chapters with next-section preview
- Heritage + community storytelling
- Restrained type
- Generous whitespace
- Progression as you scroll
- Vintage / heritage editorial mood

### Do not copy

- Golf / apparel product grids
- Cape Town / location-specific content
- Retail shop density on marketing layer
