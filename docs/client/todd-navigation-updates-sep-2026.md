## Verbatim (Todd)

NAVIGATION UPDATES

I went through the full navigation and want to simplify some of the page structure while adding a couple of things that better represent where United is going. Overall navigation design/function can stay as you currently have it. These are primarily hierarchy and page changes.

01 // ABOUT

Keep this section as-is:

Philosophy
Founder Story
Meet the Team
The Space
FAQ

All five will remain individual pages.

02 // TRAINING

Simplify/reorganize this section to:

CLASSES
BUILD
BURN
BALANCE (COMING SOON)
MOVE THE CITY // RUN CLUB

COACHING
PERSONAL TRAINING

Move the City is currently under Culture. Move it into Training and label it MOVE THE CITY // RUN CLUB so someone unfamiliar with the brand immediately understands what it is. This will have its own page.

For Personal Training, remove the separate 1-on-1 Coaching, Small Group Training and Private Group Training navigation/pages. Consolidate these into one PERSONAL TRAINING page. That page will explain the different ways someone can work with a coach, primarily 1:1 coaching and private group training, rather than creating individual pages for each option.

03 // FOUNDATION

Keep as FOUNDATION (COMING SOON) for now. This will eventually have its own dedicated page.

04 // LONGEVITY

Keep as LONGEVITY (COMING SOON) for now. This will eventually have its own dedicated page.

05 // CULTURE

Update this section to:

BY DESIGN
CULTIVATED
ARCHIVE

Remove Move the City from Culture since it is moving to Training.

Add BY DESIGN as a new page. This will focus on the intentional decisions behind the United experience: the space, equipment, materials, aesthetic, hospitality, functionality and other details that shape how United looks, feels and operates.

This is different from The Space under About. The Space shows people the physical environment; By Design explains the thinking and intention behind it.

06 // MEMBERSHIP

Keep as-is with its own dedicated page.

07 // SHOP

Keep Shop in the navigation, but this does not need a United Strength website page. Clicking Shop should take users directly to the United Limited website:

TheUnitedLimited.com

08 // START HERE

Keep as-is. This remains the primary entry point for someone interested in joining United and will lead into the application/get-started process.

OVERALL DIRECTION

The goal with these changes is to reduce unnecessary pages and make the navigation easier to understand for someone who knows nothing about United, while still giving the major parts of the brand their own place.

We don't need individual pages for every variation of a service. If multiple offerings can be clearly communicated on one page, I would rather consolidate them and keep the site intentional and easy to navigate.

Outside of these hierarchy/page changes, I like the overall navigation layout and interaction you currently have, so no need to redesign that piece.
