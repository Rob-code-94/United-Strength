# Front-End Design Skill: High-Octane Industrial Sanctuary

You are designing a professional-grade frontend interface that perfectly blends luxury editorial spacing with raw, high-performance athletic energy (inspired by the hybrid aesthetics of Sukha Club and ONE LDN). Do not use specific font families; focus completely on structural layout, geometric flow, and spatial rhythm.

## 1. The Color Archetype (Matte Dark + High-Octane Pop)
- **Base Environment:** Deep, rich matte backgrounds (true obsidian `#080808`, dark slate charcoal `#121212`). 
- **The Accent (The ONE LDN Edge):** Introduce a singular, hyper-focused, high-visibility accent color (e.g., a striking electric lime/volt green `#CCFF00` or a razor-sharp high-contrast hue). 
- **Application:** Use this intense accent *only* for ultra-minimalist elements: a tiny border indicator, a pulsing status dot, or a high-impact hover transformation. The rest of the site stays completely dark and premium.

## 2. Layout Structure (Wide-Screen Editorial Grid)
- **Wide & Full-Bleed:** Unlike standard boxed apps, use expansive, edge-to-edge section dividers and massive full-bleed visual grids that span the screen width.
- **The Spacing Balance (The Sukha Flow):** Maintain dramatic vertical padding (`py-20` to `py-32` on desktop). Do not crowd the high-performance elements; force them to breathe deeply to preserve the luxury feel.
- **Asymmetric Blocks:** Alternate between massive centered narrative banners (storytelling blocks) and razor-sharp, multi-column grid containers for services/amenities.

## 3. Structural Geometry & Lines
- **Raw Architectural Edges:** Use sharp, unrounded corners (`rounded-none`) across all structural layouts, image placeholders, and interactive elements.
- **Industrial Accents:** Implement ultra-thin, precise border lines (`border-neutral-800` or custom low-contrast separators). You may occasionally use a sharp accent-colored border line on active/hovered container blocks to simulate a raw, technical look.

## 4. Navigation & Flow Experience
- **Sleek Utility Header:** The top navigation should be a thin, expansive bar running edge-to-edge. Keep links minimal and razor-sharp. 
- **The Panel Shift:** Interactive sub-menus, filters, or class schedules must glide into view using weighted slide-out panel drawers rather than standard page-redirects or basic dropdowns.
- **High-Contrast CTAs:** Buttons must look solid and imposing—sharp rectangles that instantly command attention through stark contrast or an electric accent swap when hovered.
